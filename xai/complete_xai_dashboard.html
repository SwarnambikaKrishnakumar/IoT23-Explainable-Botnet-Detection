Explainable AI Methodology

The explainability layer combines local and global interpretation techniques to improve transparency of the anomaly detection system.

01 — LIME

Explains an individual network-flow prediction by identifying the local feature contributions that support or oppose the classification.

02 — Permutation Sensitivity

Measures the influence of individual features by observing changes in LOF prediction behavior after feature values are randomly permuted.

03 — Surrogate Model

Uses a shallow Decision Tree to approximate LOF decisions and produce human-readable decision rules.
